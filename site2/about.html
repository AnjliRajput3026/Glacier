My Story

A Decade of Compassionate Healthcare

With a Master of Arts from Rajasthan University and over ten years of frontline healthcare experience, I have built a career defined by one central purpose: ensuring that every patient feels informed, supported and cared for.

My journey has taken me through Jaipur's most respected institutions — from Vasan Eye Care to Narayana Hospital, Rungta Hospital and currently HCG Hospital — each role deepening my expertise in hospital operations, clinical coordination and patient-centred care.

I specialise in OPD management, high-volume patient coordination, medical documentation and building communication bridges between clinical teams and patients. My work consistently results in reduced wait times, smoother workflows and higher patient satisfaction.
